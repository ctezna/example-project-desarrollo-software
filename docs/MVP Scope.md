# MVP Scope

- Pre-order intake and management. **IN**
- Pickup scheduling for customer orders. **IN**
- Manual daily route planning for each food truck. **IN**
- Daily truck schedule view. **IN**
- Order status tracking (Received, Preparing, Ready, Picked Up). **IN**
- Simple inventory notes for planning stock. **IN**
- Internal access for Owner/Dispatcher, Truck Operator, and Kitchen Prep Staff. **IN**
- Optional customer order lookup. **IN**

- Online payment processing. **OUT**
- Automatic or AI-based route optimization. **OUT**

- Full restaurant POS replacement. **OUT**
