# 4. Risks, Assumptions, and Dependencies

## Risks
1. Users may resist switching from the current manual process to the new system.
2. Manual order entry may result in incorrect or incomplete information.
3. Poor daily route planning could cause delays and reduce customer satisfaction.

## Assumptions
1. Staff will have access to devices to use the application.
2. Orders will be entered accurately by staff members.
3. The owner or dispatcher will create the daily truck routes manually.

## Dependencies / Pending Decisions
1. Decide whether the customer order lookup feature will be included in the MVP.
2. Define the official order status workflow (e.g., Received, Preparing, Ready, Picked Up).
